# HTS60 source adequacy audit

The selected chains directly contain omega_b, tau, n_s and logA together with the variables
needed for the frozen DESI tangent-normal coordinates. Released weights and chain identities
support conditional covariance, burn-in and leave-one-chain-out calculations.

Eigenmode loadings are basis-dependent posterior diagnostics. Their physical interpretation is
not identifiable from these chains alone.
