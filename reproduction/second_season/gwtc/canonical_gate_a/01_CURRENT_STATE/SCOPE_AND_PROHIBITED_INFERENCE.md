# Scope and prohibited inference

## Supported claim

The frozen GWTC-4 and GWTC-5 public headline posterior products reproduce the
reported one-decimal median and 68.27% interval components under deterministic
type-7 linear percentile evaluation.

## Unsupported or prohibited claims

This branch does not establish:

- independent reproduction of the GWTC likelihood or sampler;
- independent event-level dark-siren analysis;
- independent reconstruction of the galaxy catalog or selection function;
- a new measurement of H0;
- a causal explanation of the GWTC-4 to GWTC-5 uncertainty change;
- reproduction of the published 25.7% metric from the headline posterior pair;
- resolution of the Hubble tension;
- justification for combining this posterior with another probe.

The 28.5478% headline-pair quantity is diagnostic only.
