# Public interpretation note

The original exploratory variance-component contract is preserved as a historical pre-execution record. Its motivation section used stronger language about the 72-dimensional covariance null space and a host-by-anchor interaction subspace than is warranted by the publication-level evidence alone.

For the public manuscript and current repository interpretation, the supported statement is narrower: the **observed covariance-null-space projection of the released data vector** is numerically represented by the examined host-by-anchor interaction residual coordinates to the recorded numerical precision, and the dimensions of the two 72-dimensional spaces agree. These facts do not by themselves establish equality of the full subspaces. No basis-level identity such as `N(C) = H_interaction` is claimed without an explicit projector, principal-angle, or equivalent subspace comparison.

This clarification changes no numerical result, exploratory fit, or historical execution record. It only limits the public interpretation to the evidence actually demonstrated.
