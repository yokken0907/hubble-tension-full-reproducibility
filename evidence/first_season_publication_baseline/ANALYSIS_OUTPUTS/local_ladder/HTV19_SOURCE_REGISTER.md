# Source Register

Access date: 2026-07-21 JST

## S01 Riess et al. 2022 — SH0ES global ladder

arXiv:2112.04510

Used for:

- Table 6 SN-independent Cepheid host distances;
- 42 calibrator SNe in 37 hosts and 277 Hubble-flow SNe;
- host-distance covariance with nonzero off-diagonal terms;
- common host-distance systematic scale of about 0.019 mag;
- warning that consistent SN standardization and covariance must be used.

The PDF was opened and visual screenshots of the Table 6 pages were attempted.
The screenshot service returned cache-miss errors. Parsed PDF text and ar5iv
HTML were cross-checked.

## S02 PantheonPlusSH0ES official DataRelease

Used for:

- public y, L and C matrix structure of the full ladder;
- warning that Table 2 errors omit off-diagonal covariance and are not suitable
  for an independent refit without the full matrix.

## S03 Hoyt et al. 2025 — CCHP JWST TRGB host distances

arXiv:2503.11769

Used for:

- ten JWST TRGB SN-host distances in Table 5;
- nominal homogenized F115W TRGB distance scale;
- statistical host errors excluding common zero-point calibration;
- 11-SN versus 24-SN calibrator expansion;
- different H0 movement for CSP-II and Pantheon+ magnitude sets.

## S04 Freedman et al. 2025 — CCHP status report

DOI: 10.3847/1538-4357/adce78  
arXiv:2408.06153

Used for:

- current adopted H0=70.39±1.22(stat)±1.33(sys)±0.70(SN);
- 24 HST+JWST TRGB calibrators;
- 10-host/11-SN JWST core;
- common NGC4258 zero point;
- statement that stellar distances, calibrator SNe and weights all enter the
  remaining reconciliation.

## S05 Riess et al. 2025 — current SH0ES endpoint

arXiv:2509.01667

Used for:

- H0=73.49±0.93 from JWST+HST Cepheids;
- 19 JWST hosts/24 SNe and 37 HST hosts/42 SNe;
- Cepheid+TRGB H0=73.18±0.88 using 55 calibrated SNe.

## Numerical boundary

No raw photometry, SN light curves, joint ladder matrix or MCMC was refit.

All host-level z values, weighted means and endpoint budgets are explicitly
diagonal/descriptive diagnostics.
