# Source Register

Access date: 2026-07-21 JST

## S01 Brout et al. 2022 — Pantheon+ calibration

**The Pantheon+ Analysis: SuperCal-Fragilistic Cross Calibration, Retrained
SALT2 Light Curve Model, and Calibration Systematic Uncertainty**  
arXiv:2112.03864; ApJ 938, 111

Used for:

- SuperCal and Fragilistic Table-6 controlled cases;
- distance-shape separation of SALT2 retraining and zeropoints;
- M0-surface dominance;
- CALSPEC as the largest traced M0-change cause;
- Table-5 calibration uncertainty budget;
- less-than-0.1-percent bias-correction effect in the controlled test;
- less-than-0.2 km/s/Mpc H0 calibration uncertainty;
- cancellation explanation from overlapping surveys across ladder rungs.

## S03 Brownsberger et al. 2021

**The Pantheon+ Analysis: Dependence of Cosmological Constraints on
Photometric-Zeropoint Uncertainties of Supernova Surveys**  
arXiv:2110.03486

Used for:

- independent less-than-0.2 km/s/Mpc upper-scale calibration sensitivity for
  SH0ES H0;
- explanation that shared surveys in calibrator and Hubble-flow samples make
  H0 robust to inter-survey gray offsets.

## S04 Popovic et al. 2021

**Forward-Modeling the Dust and Intrinsic Colour Distributions...**  
arXiv:2112.04456

Used for:

- separate population-model uncertainty scale
  sigma_w=0.005 and sigma_H0=0.145 km/s/Mpc;
- classification as a separate systematic rather than a Table-6 component.

## S05 Popovic et al. 2025 — Dovekie calibration

arXiv:2506.05471

Used as a later consistency check that small calibration changes may be
amplified through color-luminosity relations and SALT training.

## Boundary

No SALT2 model was retrained.

No light curves, bias simulations, cosmological likelihood or MCMC were rerun.

The Table-6 algebra is descriptive of the published controlled subset only.
