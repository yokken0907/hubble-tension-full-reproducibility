# Claim and scope boundary

## Supported bounded conclusion

A project-internal, separately written implementation was exercised first on a three-file pilot under a contract prohibiting access to prior numerical results. Its code hash, equal-weight Type-7 quantile rule, source manifest, comparison tolerance, and stopping rule were then fixed before an unchanged-code extension to 13 released HDF5 files.

Within that fixed scope:

- 13/13 structural comparisons passed;
- 39/39 q16/q50/q84 comparisons passed within the frozen tolerance;
- 12/12 Table 6 rows matched at published precision.

This strengthens implementation diversity and output-level numerical traceability for released-sample summaries.

## Historical qualification

The pilot package uses the term `blind` for a workflow contract that prohibited specified prior information. The post-pilot audit found no such values or crosswalks in the delivered code and artifacts, but the package cannot prove the absence of unrecorded exposure outside the workflow. Public summaries therefore use the neutral formulation:

> three-file pilot followed by an unchanged-code 13-file extension

The tolerance and method were pre-specified and frozen within the project before the 13-file extension. No claim of registration with an external preregistration service is made.

## Not established

This evidence does not establish:

- external independent replication;
- reproduction of the original likelihood;
- reproduction of the original sampler or posterior-generation pipeline;
- burn-in, thinning, chain convergence, autocorrelation, or effective sample size;
- correctness of the astrophysical model;
- causal attribution of posterior differences;
- a systematic associated with SLACS, SL2S, mass-sheet effects, or another component;
- a revised or preferred H0 value;
- new physics;
- resolution of the Hubble tension;
- full computational reproducibility of the TDCOSMO analysis.
