# Selection and exclusion record

## Selection principle

The two supplied TDCOSMO source packages were not copied wholesale. This directory contains only the minimum author-generated evidence needed to connect the later second-implementation result to the public provenance system and to preserve its historical predecessor state.

Selected materials were required to document one or more of:

- the three-file pilot and its information restrictions;
- implementation identity and SHA-256;
- method, tolerance, source, and stopping-rule freeze;
- the unchanged-code 13-file extension;
- 13/13 structural comparisons;
- 39/39 quantile comparisons;
- 12/12 Table 6 published-precision comparisons;
- the final `COMPLETE_WITH_SCOPE` classification;
- the interpretation boundary;
- the earlier `NOT_DONE` and HOLD chronology.

## Excluded categories

- Third-party HDF5 files: excluded from redistribution; referenced by filename, size, source commit, and SHA-256.
- Source-package ZIPs and nested result ZIPs: excluded as redundant containers after selected evidence files were extracted.
- Original paper PDF: excluded; paper identity is recorded by identifier and SHA-256 only.
- Duplicate copies of the same code, contracts, tables, and reports: one verified canonical copy retained.
- Prompt files and operational transfer instructions: excluded because they do not add public scientific evidence.
- Empty stderr logs and transient packaging files: excluded as non-substantive.
- Historical CORR1/CORR2 ZIP containers: excluded because their selected historical records already exist in `ANALYSIS_OUTPUTS/tdcosmo/`.
- Third-party source bytes with unclear redistribution scope: excluded.

A member-level disposition list was retained in the private build records and is not part of the public repository release. The public exclusion rationale is summarized in this document.
