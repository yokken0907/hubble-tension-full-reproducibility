## H0DN scope

For a residual strictly in the support of a symmetric positive-semidefinite covariance, the target-dependent singular-Gaussian quadratic is invariant under an invertible row-coordinate transformation. The observed H0DN transformation result concerns a different setting: the design is nearly in covariance support, the rounded public data vector has an off-support component, and the Moore–Penrose quadratic is used as a projected loss. The measured non-invariance is tied to that off-support projected-loss extension.

The result does not establish that arbitrary singular Gaussian inference is non-invariant. It does not define a preferred row representation, corrected covariance, corrected H0, pipeline error, or physical cause.
