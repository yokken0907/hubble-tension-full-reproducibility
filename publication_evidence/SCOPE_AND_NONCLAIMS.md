## H0DN scope

The observed H0DN transformation result concerns a different setting: the design is nearly in covariance support, the rounded public data vector has an off-support component, and the Moore–Penrose quadratic is used as a projected loss. The measured non-invariance is tied to that off-support projected-loss extension.
