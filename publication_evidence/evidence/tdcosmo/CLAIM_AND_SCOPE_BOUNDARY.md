# Claim and Scope Boundary

## Supported bounded conclusion

A project-internal, separately written implementation, frozen after a blind
three-file sentinel, recovered the source-file structure and equal-weight H0
quantiles for the twelve flat-LambdaCDM Table 6 chains plus one
identifier-collision control within a pre-registered numerical tolerance.

The result strengthens output-level traceability and implementation diversity
for this bounded calculation.

## Not established

This branch does not establish:

- an external independent replication;
- reproduction of the original likelihood;
- reproduction of the original sampler or posterior-generation pipeline;
- chain convergence, autocorrelation, or effective-sample-size adequacy;
- correctness of the astrophysical model;
- causal attribution of posterior differences;
- evidence that SLACS, SL2S, mass-sheet effects, or another systematic explain
  the Hubble tension;
- a revised or preferred value of H0;
- a solution to the Hubble tension;
- full computational reproducibility of the TDCOSMO analysis.

## Terminology

Permitted:

- `project-internal alternate implementation`
- `implementation-diversity check`
- `output-level numerical traceability`
- `complete with scope`

Not permitted:

- `external independent replication`
- `full reproduction`
- `TDCOSMO confirmed`
- `H0 corrected`
- `Hubble tension explained`
