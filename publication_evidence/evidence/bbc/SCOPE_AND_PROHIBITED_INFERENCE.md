# Scope and prohibited inference

## Permitted claim

At the fixed official repository commit, the public materials inspected were
insufficient to uniquely define a reproducible Pantheon+ BBC truth-level
closure using matched truth, fit, pre/post correction vectors, covariance,
configuration, and byte-level provenance.

## Prohibited claims

This audit does **not** establish that:

- Pantheon+ BBC corrections are incorrect;
- the missing products do not exist internally;
- the public final distance vector or covariance is invalid;
- the Hubble constant inferred by Pantheon+ or SH0ES is biased;
- the ten public simulations are equivalent to the large BiasCor training
  products;
- a private file supplied without a public versioned record would satisfy the
  public-reproducibility requirement.
